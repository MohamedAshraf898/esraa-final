What can you design?+

Social media posts and campaigns, brand identities, print materials, and short-form video content (reels).

What software do you work in?+

Adobe Photoshop and the Adobe Creative Suite, with a strong focus on typography and layout.

What do you need from me to start?+

Your brand assets (logo, colors, fonts if you have them), a brief on the message, and any reference designs you like.

Usually within a few days of agreeing on the brief — turnaround depends on the size of the campaign.
